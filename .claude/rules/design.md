A colour means one thing board-wide, one fact rides one element, and shape is spent before colour. Every mark
has a row in the `Help` legend, rendered as the page's own element — a mark with no row is one a human guesses.

The palette, the mark vocabulary and the metrics: **`docs/rules/design.md`** — read it before adding or
recolouring a mark.
