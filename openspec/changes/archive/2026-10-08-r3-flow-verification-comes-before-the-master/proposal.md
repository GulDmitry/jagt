## Why

A hand-back held at VERIFYING told the session "the Master reads this round next" and the human "with the Master",
though a red run goes back to the session and the Master reads nothing. Two predicates answered whether the Master
reads a status.

## What Changes

- At VERIFYING the session and the human are told verification runs first.
- One set in `FlowRules` says which statuses the Master reads.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `task-flow`: verification comes before the Master's read.
