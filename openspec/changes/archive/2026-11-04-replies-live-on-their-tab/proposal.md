## Why

The card's `Replies` button and the `Drafted review` tab showed the same `review_replies.md` under two names.

## What Changes

- An offer whose `part` names a tab is not drawn on the card; the replies live on their tab.
- The tab wears the your-move mark while the replies were written since it was last opened, kept in this browser only.
- The legend's replies row renders that mark.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `review`: the replies report's only button is the tab.
- `board`: a verb declares its tab, not a card button.
- `board-workspace`: the Drafted review tab and its mark.
