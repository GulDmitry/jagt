## Why

`attention` is at its page budget; the routing of points to channels is a subject of its own.

## What Changes

- The two requirements on points, channels and a role's points move, text unchanged, from `attention` to `notification-routing`.
- Five over-long `attention` requirements are split into shorter ones, no fact dropped.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `notification-routing`: a point routed to channels, told once per key; a role's points.

### Modified Capabilities

- `attention`: loses the two routing requirements.
