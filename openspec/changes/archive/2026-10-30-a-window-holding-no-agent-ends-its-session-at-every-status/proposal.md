## Why

A session whose agent process died ended only at `NEW`, ship asked the window on its own, and a quiet session whose process ran said nothing of it.

## What Changes

- A window holding no agent process ends the session at every open status, with the agent's last lines.
- The window state is one sign of the session record; ship, the session-alive stop and the restart ask answer from it, and an unreadable window answers unknown.
- A quiet session whose process runs says so.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `attention`: the read carries the window's process and ends the session by it.
