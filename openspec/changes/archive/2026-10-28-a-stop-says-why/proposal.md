## Why

A stopped card asked `lift the stop` and its hover held no evidence: nobody could tell why, or whether lifting was safe.

## What Changes

- Each stop trigger has one short reason; the card's move line reads `lift the stop — <reason>`.
- That line's hover shows the evidence, the trigger's advice and the stop's time in UTC.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `unattended-work`: the stopped-task requirement names what the line and its hover say.
