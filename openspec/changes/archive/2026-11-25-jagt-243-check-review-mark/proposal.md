## Why

A `sweep` that finds nothing new, or whose round still waits, answered with a toast alone: it vanished and the card said nothing about the press.

## What Changes

- A press that did nothing answers `quiet` beside its sentence, and its button wears a dashed muted edge.
- The mark ends when the card's state moves or the verb is pressed again.
- The `Help` legend gains the mark's row.
