## Why

On a stand run in `act`, a thread the session declined with evidence, and the reviewer never answered, was relayed every
two minutes for twenty: the posted decline made the thread read new, so every poll was a round.

## What Changes

- The review read names the account the host's tools act as; a thread whose newest note is by it waits on the
  reviewer: it is not relayed, keeps the task from REVIEWED, and only a new note on it starts a round.
- A review round relayed to the session counts toward the rounds-running ceiling as other rounds do.
