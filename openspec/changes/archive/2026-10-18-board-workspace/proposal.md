## Why

A grid of cards scattered the tasks that waited on the human, showed nothing on a click, and said nothing to a
window behind another one.

## What Changes

- Tasks are rows in a list, the human's own in a band on top; a picked row opens in a pane beside it as its card.
- The tab's title counts the tasks waiting on the human, and its icon wears a dot.
- A row whose state moved while it was closed is bold until opened.
- `j`/`k` walk the list, `n` opens the next task waiting on the human, a verb's own key runs it on the open one.
- A flagged row leads its group.
- The header holds only what needs a look: the count, a failed job, the Master while it acts, the filter, Reports, New task.
- Every job shows as a row with a Run now.
- A project is added from the board, its path read as it is typed.
- The open task has three tabs: history, the session's screen, the drafted review replies.
- A verb carries its key on its button, and Done stands apart.
- Position carries the tier: the human's tasks first, each group in registration order.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `board`: a list and a pane in place of the card grid.
