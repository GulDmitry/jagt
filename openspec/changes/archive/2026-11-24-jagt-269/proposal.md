## Why

The Master pressed ship over eight drafted replies to people's notes. The status left the hand-back, the card dropped the drafts, and the human saw no reaction to the open threads.

## What Changes

- The Master in act does not ship while drafts wait that a ship will not post, and by default none to people.
- `codeReview.repliesToPeopleUnasked` (default `false`) lets it ship and post; each posted reply opens with the AI line.
- Drafts no ship posts stay pending on the card, whatever the status, until the human posts or ends them.
- The your-move notification says drafted replies wait, a round with no code change included.
- A round relays a thread's notes newer than the answer posted on it, by hand or from a draft.
