## Why

A bad practice the rules name is refused by the build at its first new use, not found in review; an exception needs more than its author.

## What Changes

- A row may carry `exception: "<number>"`, naming the decision that grants its exceptions.
- A decision granting an exception holds the owner's approval and a reviewer's agreement in its Status.
- `Thread.sleep` in main is a ratchet at its present count.
