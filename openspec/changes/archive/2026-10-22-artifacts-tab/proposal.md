## Why

The documents of a task are `Artifact`s in the code and in the decision record; the tab called them "Files" and read as a worktree browser, so the plan and the visual went unfound.
The tabs stood in the order they were built; the human opens the plan and the round far more often than the session, which `focus` reaches anyway.

## What Changes

- The `Files` tab is the `Artifacts` tab.
- The tabs stand in the order Overview, Review round, Artifacts, Session.
