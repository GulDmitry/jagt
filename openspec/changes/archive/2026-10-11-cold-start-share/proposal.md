## Why

A prompt prefix that stops matching the cache bills every call at the write price; nothing showed it.

## What Changes

- `stats` adds a `COLD` column: starts that read no cache over starts measured, `?` where unmeasured.
- A start is a headless call, or the first turn of a session log; the column replaces nothing and adds no mark.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `board`: the `stats` report carries the cold share.
