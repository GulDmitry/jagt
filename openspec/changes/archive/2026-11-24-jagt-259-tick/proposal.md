## Why

A hand-back in the minute before the pre-ship read could ship its change unreviewed.

## What Changes

- SHIP is declined until the pre-ship read of the current tree has run, with the sentence naming what ends it.
- A failed read, a reviewer switched off or an empty diff never holds a ship.
