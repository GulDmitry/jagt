## Why

The session's standing rules were a template inside the jar, `sub-agent-context.md`, that no human could edit and
whose fifteen positional slots a moved line would scramble.

## What Changes

- The rules are the brief's `context` step; jagt writes the task's facts above them and `<hard_safety>` below.
- `<hard_safety>` stays jagt's: a human's copy of the brief cannot loosen what its guards enforce.
