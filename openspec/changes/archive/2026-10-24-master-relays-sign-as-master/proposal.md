## Why

The Master's verdicts relayed with no origin, so a miss they set off was recorded as `jagt` and the move they took
carried no asker.

## What Changes

- A verdict and an answer of the Master's run as `MASTER`: a miss reads `by: master`, the move it takes is signed
  `MASTER` in the task's history.
