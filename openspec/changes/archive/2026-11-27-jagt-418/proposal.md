## Why

A move with no edge for a state was ignored the same way whether that was meant or forgotten; seven status-landing defects came from it.

## What Changes

- Every kind of move names the regions it is the concern of, and every state of those regions takes it, ignores it with its why or refuses it under a name.
- Building the chart fails on a state with none; the explorer asserts every reachable pair has a class.
- A fact move with no class in production logs `move unclassified` and marks the board's head; a claim or a press is refused.
- A round read and a pipeline read on the deploy commit answer empty where the chart ignores them, never the status they met.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `task-flow`: every kind of move has a class in every state; two scenario groups folded to make room.
