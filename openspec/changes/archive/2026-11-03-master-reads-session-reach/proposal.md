## Why

The Master's reads were fenced to the task's worktrees, so a project whose sessions write a spec repository beside them
had every plan and round end `not ready`, asking the human what the reader could not open.

## What Changes

- A plan, round or question read also reads each present directory `agent.sandbox.allowWrite` names, sandbox on or off.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `master`: a read reaches what a session may write beside its worktrees.
