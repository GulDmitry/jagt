## Why

Logic that is hard to follow from its code, such as rules that combine or rows and columns that shift each other, is
understood faster from a worked example than from the code.

## What Changes

- With visualize on, the session counts such logic as worth a picture and draws a worked example, its input and its outcome.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `session-brief`: the visualize criterion and the picture a task gets include hard-to-follow logic.
