## Why

Where the Master acts, a stalled deploy conflict told the human at once, though the Master answers every other question a session stops on.

## What Changes

- In `act`, the first stall of a handed conflict is put to the Master as the session's question at `DEPLOY_CONFLICT`;
  the `Conflict` records it, and the stall clock restarts.
- A second stall on that conflict, or any stall where the Master may not answer, tells the human once, as before.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `task-flow`: a stalled deploy conflict goes to the Master first where it answers, then to the human.
