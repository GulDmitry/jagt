## Why

Projects stood in the order they first appeared, so where a project landed in the task list or the launch dialog
depended on which task came first and on the order of `jagt.yml`.

## What Changes

- The task list groups projects by name, and the launch dialog offers its boxes by name.
- A launch still sends the ticked projects in `jagt.yml` order, whose first is the task's primary repository.
