## Why

The board labels its shortcuts `⌘K` on every platform, a key a Linux or Windows keyboard does not have.

## What Changes

- A shortcut's label names the modifier of the human's platform: `⌘` on Apple, `Ctrl+` elsewhere.
- The catalogs write `{mod}` where they wrote `⌘`.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `board-workspace`: a shortcut's label follows the platform.
