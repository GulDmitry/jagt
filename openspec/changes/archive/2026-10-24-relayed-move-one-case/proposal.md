## Why

A step the Master relays carried its statuses twice, in its session case and in `master-cases.md`, and the door
checked both: two files for one fact, which a copy could set apart.

## What Changes

- `master-cases.md.dist` keeps the verbs the Master presses (`ship`, `reply`, `deploy`, `approve-plan`, `add-repo`).
- A step the Master relays takes its `at` from its session case `by: master` alone.
- Startup refuses a Master case naming a step it relays, and a step it relays no session case sets off `by: master`.
