## Why

A session verifying on a stage asks questions and changes no code, so the tree stays unchanged by nature. From the
third answer on, every answer killed the session and started a fresh one: four restarts in seven minutes, each
losing what the session had already tried.

## What Changes

- A Master answer never starts a fresh session.
- Past three answers over an unchanged tree the Master is still told its answers are not acted on; the ninth stays a runaway.

### Modified Capabilities

- `master`: the repeated-question scenario.
